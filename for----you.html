I / The record

What stayed.

I couldn’t tell you what day it was. If I wrote a date at the top of this, I’d have made one up. I remember her face. I remember how quickly I could find it again in a crowd of other faces.

There’s a green I think of when I try to explain it. The green deep in an emerald when you hold it at an angle to the window. The room can be dull and there it is anyway.

I wanted to put that somewhere I could find again.

II / The photographs

The pictures I kept.

I still have every one of them. I can open almost any photograph here and know which one I want to see next.

III / An unsent letter

Elisha,

I know so little about your ordinary days. I can’t tell you which mug you reach for in the morning, or whether you finish your coffee before it gets cold. I hope there’s someone who knows and puts the right mug out for you.

It feels good to think of you going on with your day somewhere while I sit here writing this. We have never said hello. I can wish you a good morning all the same.

If one day you forget having stood for any of these photographs, that would make sense. You’ve had a whole life since each one. I just happened to remember.

I hope today was kind to you.

IV / The distance

On my side of the door.

Sometimes I’ve pictured a door and wondered what I would say if it opened. I never get very far with that picture. Years of having something to say and I think I’d still struggle with hello.

I can be patient for years at this distance. A person sitting next to me can tell when I’m only half listening. Both of those things are true of me. I want the person next to me to get more of that patience.

If you’re reading this and there’s someone close enough to hear you, tell them the thing you came here hoping to find a way to say. Use the words you already have.

V / At the end

The light can stay.

I’ve spent a long time trying to get this right. If I come across it years from now, I hope I can remember why I cared how it sounded.

Of all the things I’ve put here, there’s one I want to be able to read back without having to guess what I meant.

I love her.

For Elisha.

From a stranger who kept the light on.
